# Dữ liệu / Data

Tải Online Retail II từ https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii
Đặt `online_retail_II.xlsx` tại `data/raw/`, rồi chạy `python src/prepare_retail.py` từ project root.

Source: Chen, D. (2012), Online Retail II, UCI, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D, CC BY 4.0.
Publisher describes prices in sterling (GBP), 1,067,371 invoice lines, 01/12/2009–09/12/2011.
The local workbook has matching name, row count, sheets and date range; its bytes have not been compared against a fresh publisher download.

Workbook and raw/processed records are not bundled. Aggregated outputs are included for inspection.
